## The two mirrors, and why they land in exactly these places

`lisp.h` 2289 includes `thread.h` unconditionally, so `lisp.rs` includes `thread.rs` at the
point its own `lisp.h 2290` section starts; `data.c` reaches `font.h` through
`keyboard.h` 23 → `coding.h` 284 → `composite.h` 28, so `data.rs`'s include list puts
`font.rs` immediately before `composite.rs`.  Both mirrors are WIP (their ledgers keep
unchecked boxes) and carry only what this file needs: `THREADP`/`MUTEXP`/`CONDVARP`, and
`FONTP` plus the three font predicates with `enum font_property_index`'s members.  Those
members are `pub const`s rather than a Rust enum because two of them are written as other
members (`FONT_OBJLIST_INDEX = FONT_SPEC_MAX`, `FONT_NAME_INDEX = FONT_ENTITY_MAX`), which a
Rust enum discriminant cannot name.

## Two mirror bugs this round surfaced

1. `PSEUDOVECTOR_TYPE`'s mirror said `*mut vectorlike_header` and read `(*v).size`; lisp.h
   1784 takes `const struct Lisp_Vector *` and reads `v->header.size`.  data.c's
   `cl-type-of` is the first caller, so the probe had never been exercised: both the
   signature and the body are now C's.
2. `Fcl_type_of` also needed `XSUBR (...)->max_args` compared against `UNEVALLED`, which the
   port spells with an `as isize` (the `subr-arity` precedent).

## P-76 is fixed, and that is what closes the file

PORTING.md P-76 recorded that `extract-abi.py` read `defsubr` calls from raw text, so
`data.c`'s `#ifdef`-guarded registrations (`Suser_ptrp` under `HAVE_MODULES`, three under
`HAVE_NATIVE_COMP`) were demanded of the port even though the frozen configuration never
compiles them.  `extract-abi.py`'s defsubr extraction now runs `ci.dead_branch_map` over the
comment-blanked text and skips calls inside untaken branches — the fix P-76's own row
described.  The row is flipped to 已修 with this round as its evidence, and the temporary
"record it in the ledger" advice is withdrawn.  With that, `syms_of_data` states all 116
live registrations and the four dead ones are documented instead of demanded.
