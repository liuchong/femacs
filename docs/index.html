Current status

Standalone Lisp crates lisp-vm, lisp-std, lisp, and elisp (v0.0.1) accompany the Apple silicon terminal and Cocoa trial builds from the November 20, 2026 modernization milestone. macOS and Debian 12 aarch64 VM runtimes are verified; Windows is cross-compile architecture verified only.

独立 Lisp 运行时四件套 lisp-vm、lisp-std、lisp、elisp（v0.0.1）与 2026 年 11 月 20 日结项的 Apple Silicon 终端及 Cocoa 试用构建现已同步发布。已在 macOS 真机与 Debian 12 aarch64 虚拟机完成运行态验证；Windows 仅完成交叉编译架构验证。
